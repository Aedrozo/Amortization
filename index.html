For illustration only — this is not an offer or a commitment to lend. The figures produced by this calculator are estimates generated from the information you enter. They are not an advertisement of credit terms, a loan approval, a rate lock, a Loan Estimate, or a commitment to lend. Rates, fees and terms shown are those you typed in; actual available rates change daily and depend on credit, income, assets, occupancy, property type, loan-to-value and other factors. All loans are subject to credit approval, underwriting, and property appraisal.

Interest rate is not APR. The rate you enter is a note rate. The Annual Percentage Rate reflects the note rate plus prepaid finance charges such as points and lender fees, and will be higher whenever those costs exist. Where you supply those costs, this tool estimates an APR under the method in Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.22); that estimate is not a substitute for the APR disclosed on your official Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure.

Assumptions. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and mortgage insurance are estimates — your escrow amounts are set by your taxing authority, insurer and association and can change over time. Interest accrues monthly and assumes every payment is made on schedule; results assume a fixed rate for the full term and do not model adjustable-rate adjustments, interest-only periods, temporary buydown subsidies, balloon features, or prepayment penalties. Mortgage insurance cancellation follows the Homeowners Protection Act using your original property value; your servicer's requirements may differ, and lender-paid or FHA mortgage insurance may not cancel at all. Refinance results exclude prepaid interest, escrow reserves and per-diem charges collected at closing. Refinancing may increase the total cost of your loan over its life. Consult a tax advisor regarding the deductibility of interest.
